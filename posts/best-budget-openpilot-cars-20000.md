# What You Should Check Before Buying

Before purchasing any used openpilot-compatible vehicle, verify:

## Driver Assistance Package

Many vehicles only support openpilot if they include:
- Adaptive cruise control
- Lane keeping assist
- Specific camera or radar packages

Always double-check the exact trim and options.

## Tire Condition

Modern driver assistance systems depend heavily on good tires. Worn or cheap tires can noticeably hurt lane-centering confidence and ride quality.

## Windshield Replacement History

Some cars require expensive recalibration after windshield replacement. Poor recalibration can affect ADAS performance.

## Battery Health (For EVs and Hybrids)

For EVs:
- Request battery health data if possible
- Verify charging speed
- Check recall history

For hybrids:
- Confirm smooth hybrid operation
- Listen for cooling fan noise from battery systems

# Bonus Tips for Finding Good Deals

## Search Outside Major Cities

Cars located slightly outside major metro areas are often:
- Cheaper
- Less competitive
- Better maintained

## Prioritize Single-Owner Cars

Vehicles with:
- Complete service history
- Fewer owners
- Highway mileage

…are often far better long-term buys than lower-mileage city cars with poor maintenance records.

## Don’t Fear Higher Hybrid Mileage

Toyota hybrids regularly exceed:
- 200,000 miles
- 250,000 miles
- Even 300,000+ miles

…with proper maintenance.

A well-maintained Prius with 140k miles can easily be a smarter purchase than a neglected crossover with 70k.

## Consider Insurance Costs

Before buying:
- Get insurance quotes
- Compare hybrid vs EV pricing
- Check registration fees

Some EVs can surprise buyers with higher insurance premiums despite low operating costs.
